Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

NeuroCardioAI is committed to protecting the privacy and security of user data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and health-related information when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Demographic Data (Age, Gender, Address)
  • Health Parameters (Blood Pressure, Glucose Level, Medical History)
  • Medical Images (MRI scans if uploaded)
  • System Usage and Interaction Data

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected data is used to:

  • Generate AI-based stroke risk predictions
  • Improve model performance and system accuracy
  • Provide personalized health insights
  • Enhance platform security and user experience
  • Support academic and medical research (anonymized data only)

4. Data Security

We implement strong security measures including encrypted data transmission, secure database storage, access control mechanisms, and periodic security audits to safeguard user information.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

NeuroCardioAI does NOT sell or rent personal data. Information may be shared only:

  • When required by legal authorities
  • For approved medical research using anonymized datasets
  • To maintain system functionality and security

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and session technologies to enhance user experience, maintain secure login sessions, and analyze system usage trends.

7. User Rights

  • Access and update personal data
  • Request deletion of account information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Contact support regarding privacy concerns

8. AI & Medical Data Disclaimer

Health predictions generated by NeuroCardioAI are based on Artificial Intelligence models and may not be fully accurate. Users should always consult qualified healthcare professionals before making medical decisions.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect technological, legal, or operational changes. Continued use of the platform signifies acceptance of updated policies.
