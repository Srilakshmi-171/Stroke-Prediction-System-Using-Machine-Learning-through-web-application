About Grad-CAM

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) is a technique that produces visual explanations for CNN-based models. It highlights the regions in the image that are most important for the model's prediction.

  • Red areas: High importance regions
  • Blue areas: Low importance regions
  • Green areas: Moderate importance

This helps doctors identify potential areas of concern in medical images.
